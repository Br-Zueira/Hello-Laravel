### Project Context & Progression
I started brainstorming and prototyping the initial core engine of this project shortly before discovering Stardance and creating the GitHub repository. At that stage, the project was completely raw and missing the vast majority of its current features. 

Approximately **50% to 70% of the entire codebase**—including the complete retro-terminal frontend styling, full database CRUD endpoints, secure CSRF routing, TomSelect integrations, mobile optimization, and overall production polishment—was built entirely from scratch during my hours logged for Stardance.
